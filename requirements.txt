# unbounded `fastmcp>=2.0.0` resolves to fastmcp 4.x and drags mcp 2.1.1 in

# not installed cannot drag anything in. foxxe-mcp bounds `mcp` directly.

# NO foxxe-mcp installed, on mcp 1.29.1 with fastmcp 3.4.7 — the dependency
# commits were never redeployed, exactly as fleet.toml warned.
